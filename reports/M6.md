## What was built

**Two dashboards.** "Compare experiments" puts the runs side by side: what each run was, its numbers,
which pairs of runs can fairly be compared, and one signal drawn for all runs on "minutes since
start". "Experiment detail" shows one run: its phases, one signal with its excluded readings, the
electrical and capture series, and the quality tables.

Details: files `grafana/dashboards/compare.json` (uid `dac-compare`, 9 panels) and
`grafana/dashboards/experiment.json` (uid `dac-experiment`, 10 panels and one annotation), folder
"DAC". Provider settings `allowUiUpdates: false` and `disableDeletion: true`. Datasources
`influx-dac` (InfluxDB SQL over Flight, database `dac_raw`) and `pg-dac` (Postgres), provisioned from
`grafana/provisioning/`. No dashboard writes to either store.

**One shared clock, made in the query.** Each experiment starts on a different date. The overlay
subtracts each experiment's own start, taken from Postgres, from every reading's time. So the six
lines start at zero together and no copy of the data is stored.

Details: a hidden variable `windows` lists the selected experiments with their start and end in
nanoseconds; the InfluxDB query joins on it and returns `experiment_time_min`. Full resolution, at
most 6 x 7,200 points. The XY chart must use `mapping: auto` with `series: []`: with the other keys
Grafana 13 draws only the first experiment (found in a rendered screenshot; a test pins it).

**Steady numbers and pair comparability in Postgres.** The comparison step wrote its results to a
file. A small loader now copies them into three Postgres tables so Grafana can show them.

Details: migration `0004_comparison.sql` (tables `comparison_kpi`, `comparison_pair`,
`comparison_pair_kpi`) and `python -m scripts.load_comparison`. Real run: 30 KPI rows, 15 pairs, 75
pair KPIs. One transaction per version, counts read back, exit codes 0, 2, 3, 4. Described in
[`docs/schema.md`](../docs/schema.md).

**A checker that runs every panel query.** `python -m scripts.check_dashboards` reads the dashboard
files, runs every panel and annotation query through Grafana for each experiment and signal, and
writes what it found. It is what replaces clicking through the dashboards as the evidence.

Details: a static lint (datasource ids, variables declared before use, unique panel ids), then a live
run through `/api/ds/query`. Exit 0 every query returned rows (allowed titles exempt), 1 one was
empty or failed, 2 refused, nothing written. Contract:
`contracts/dashboard_check.schema.json`.

## Where it differs from the plan

Each item is a limit a reader should know about. All are in the dashboard decision record.

1. **One colour for annotation regions.** Grafana 13 cannot colour each region by its kind. The plan
   asked for grey gaps and orange flatlines on the plot. The plot has one amber marking; grey and
   amber appear in the "kind" column of the gaps table.

   Details: the annotation is one entry limited to the signal panel, with the kind in its text and
   tags. Two annotations (one per kind) would give two colours on the plot; not built.

2. **A database with no gap at all shows an error in two places.** InfluxDB creates a table with its
   first row, so a database with no gap and no stuck stretch has no `quality_intervals` table. The
   gaps table and the annotation then fail instead of showing "none". The real data has 34 intervals
   and is not affected.

   Details: the compare test fixture is such a case; its test writes one row for a made-up
   experiment id so the table is empty rather than failing. EXP_005 is empty on real data but the
   table exists.

3. **Bad and uncertain thresholds are my reading.** The plan gave colour steps for completeness and
   valid share only. I mirrored them for the bad and uncertain share (green below 0.05, amber below
   0.5, red above).

   Details: a display choice, listed in the decision record. The 0.05 and 0.5 are not a policy.

4. **The first compare table is two rows taller than planned.** A render clipped it to 4 of the 6
   experiments. I raised it from 6 to 8 grid rows and shifted the panels below; no query changed.

5. **The colour steps are display only.** Nothing is stored or decided from them. The 0.95 equals the
   metrics step's "complete" limit, and a test checks that they stay equal.

## Decisions for your review

**1. Dashboards are files, not clicked together.** This was approved with the plan. It replaces the
old rule "build in the browser, export at the end" from the provisioning decision. Recommendation:
keep it; nobody builds these dashboards by hand.

Details: the old rule has an addendum in the provisioning decision record. The cost is that a change
means editing JSON and rerunning the checker.

**2. Images are pinned.** Grafana 13.3.2 and the renderer v5.12.11, with their digests, recorded in
the decision record. Version 13 was chosen over an untested 12.4.16. Recommendation: accept.

Details: Grafana 13 refuses to start with a renderer and the default token, so a renderer token is
set from `.env` (`GRAFANA_RENDERER_TOKEN`).

**3. The wide time picker question is closed for this data set.** On the real data the wide picker
gave the same rows as the experiment window. At plant scale that is not safe to assume.
Recommendation: accept the closure with the caveat; a months-wide picker should read the 1-minute
rollup.

Details: the assumptions file carries the caveat and the evidence. No panel reads the rollup yet.

## Things to know

**The EXP_004 air-flow screenshot looks odd, and it is the data.** The red excluded points sit near
zero while the accepted line before 09:00 is near 1200. This is not a unit mix-up. The raw air flow
after 09:00Z really is near 20 (the comparison step found a median of 20.0), and the cleaning step
excluded that hour as a flat stretch. Zoom the time picker into 09:00 to 10:00 and the points
separate from the bottom edge.

Details: 3,600 excluded readings, all reason `flatline`. On a plot that runs to 1400, a value of 20
is a line along the bottom.

**No panel states a finding.** The comparability table and the steady-state numbers are the committed
results of the comparison step, not conclusions. The flow and pressure against voltage answer is not
on any panel.

Details: this follows the working contract: that answer is a headline claim and waits for your
approval of the draft findings. A voltage-against-flow panel would come after that approval.
