## What changed, in plain words

- **One clock.** Every reading gets a time in seconds since its experiment's start. Phases
  (startup, steady state, load change, shutdown) are kept apart, and the first 60 s of each phase
  are skipped.
- **A voltage model per experiment.** Voltage is fitted on current, electrolyte temperature and two
  more terms, in one regression, so flow and pressure are judged after current and temperature are
  taken out. Intervals come from resampling 2-minute blocks of the run's own readings.
- **An evidence rule that can say "not resolved".** A term is "supported" only when at least two
  experiments exclude zero and agree on the sign.
- **A comparability check.** Seven conditions are measured for every pair. A pair is comparable
  when exactly one differs.
- **Two small checks.** Does pressure drop follow air flow, and does the EXP_004 air-flow hour look
  like a sensor fault.
- **Draft findings and a method document.** Both are checked by tests against the code or the
  committed results.

Details: new files `pipeline/compare.py`, `pipeline/compare_store.py`, `pipeline/compare_run.py`,
`scripts/compare.py`, the contract `contracts/comparison_summary.schema.json` with an example, the
fixture `tests/fixtures/compare/`, and the document and findings tests. The command only reads; it
writes `reports/comparison_summary.json` and `reports/comparison_report.md` at the end, and only
when it exits 0. Exit codes: 0 written; 2 refused, nothing written (including a database that does
not exist, which it never creates); 3 a query failed. CI gains a step that validates the contract
example.

## What you should look at

Each has a recommendation.

1. **The answer is "not resolved".** In this run the data does not show an effect of flow or
   pressure on cell voltage, and it does not show their absence. Recommendation: approve the
   wording as it is, and present it as "the data cannot say, here is why", not as "no effect".

   Details: hydrolyzer steady, flow and pressure intervals include zero in 6 of 6 experiments;
   pooled flow -0.0068 [-0.0328, 0.0156] V per L/min, pooled pressure 0.200 [-0.044, 0.455] V per
   bar. Removing both terms lowers R squared by at most 1.39e-05 in a steady fit. Whole-run fits
   are not separable (flow VIF 14 to 29, pooled 18.0), so they are not read. The absorber is also
   `not_resolved`: air flow 1 of 6, pressure drop 2 of 6 exclude zero, and the pooled
   pressure-drop sign (positive) is the opposite of the two experiments that exclude zero
   (negative).

2. **EXP_005's intervals are probably too narrow.** Its residuals are strongly correlated from one
   2-minute block to the next, so the bootstrap treats them as more independent than they are. Its
   "excludes zero" results (one hydrolyzer whole-run flow interval, one absorber pressure-drop
   interval) are the weakest. Recommendation: keep them in the tables, and do not lean on them.

   Details: the lag-1 correlation of the block residuals (hydrolyzer model, steady windows) is 0.87
   for EXP_005, against -0.15 to 0.12 for the other five. This is from the execute log, not the
   summary. Measured on the hydrolyzer fit only; the absorber fit of EXP_005 was not measured.
   EXP_005 also has a varying current setpoint (solar tracking) and a steady-window flow VIF of
   14.3, so even its steady fit is not separable.

3. **The EXP_004 air-flow drop looks like a sensor fault.** After 09:00Z the raw air flow falls by
   98.3 % while fan speed, pressure drop, outlet CO2 and capture efficiency do not move.
   Recommendation: accept the status update of the air-flow assumption (A-07) to "supported, still
   an assumption", since no second instrument exists.

   Details: medians before and after, with 3600 raw points on each side. The pressure drop did not
   fall either, but finding 4 shows pressure drop follows air flow only across experiments, so
   that is weaker support than fan speed and CO2.

4. **Only one pair can be compared: EXP_001 against EXP_002 (setpoint 150 A against 250 A).**
   The other 14 differ in two or more conditions. Recommendation: accept; present the supported
   pair's gaps as sizes, not as exact values, because EXP_002 has partial coverage on voltage,
   power and energy per kilogram.

   Details: the tolerances are judgement (5 percent on setpoints and flows, 5 degrees Celsius
   ambient, 15 percentage points humidity); the measured values are in the report so a reader can
   disagree. A missing value (EXP_005's batch, EXP_006's operator) counts as differing for the
   batch and is only listed for the operator.

5. **Pressure drop does not follow air flow within an experiment.** The data dictionary says it
   scales with air flow. Within each experiment R squared is at most 0.002, and a lag of up to 60 s
   does not help. Across experiments it follows, but only on two air-flow levels (1200 and 2400
   m3/h, the latter in EXP_003 alone). Recommendation: do not cite the pressure-drop law from this
   data.

   Details: slope 0.0381 mbar per m3/h and R squared 0.999998 across the six medians. A line
   through two clusters always fits.

6. **Partial coverage on capture numbers.** EXP_004, EXP_005 and EXP_006 capture and
   energy-per-kilogram rest on 0.475, 0.735 and 0.266 of their steady seconds. Recommendation:
   accept; these come from earlier cleaning and gap calls, not from this step.

   Details: the causes are in `reports/M4.md` (item 5): EXP_004's excluded air-flow hour, EXP_005's
   uncertain ambient pressure, EXP_006's stuck outlet CO2 and gap.

7. **A reading choice that moves a number a little.** Steady KPIs use only instants inside the
   steady windows. Reading the plan literally would bridge the last second of each window to the
   first reading of the next phase. Recommendation: keep it; it keeps transient readings out.

   Details: on the test fixture coverage is 838 of 840 seconds instead of 840 of 840 and the mean
   power is 12.1090 kW instead of 12.1106; on real data about 1 s per window edge (about 0.2
   percent).

8. **The absorber fits cannot be tested on the test fixture.** The fixture's noise rule makes
   current and air flow constant at the instants of the pressure drop, so the absorber model is
   singular there by construction. Unit tests cover the absorber path, and on the real data all 14
   absorber fits are testable. Recommendation: accept, or ask for a fixture change at the next plan.

   Details: found when the fixture was built; changing the fixture is a plan matter, so it was left
   as it is. The absorber evidence in the fixture run is `not_testable`.

## Known limits

| Limit | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| One run per condition (A-09) | Every difference is "in this run". Intervals show noise inside a run, not the spread between days. |
| EXP_005 autocorrelation | Its intervals are probably too narrow. |
| The air-flow sensor fault is an assumption (A-07) | The check supports it; there is no second instrument. |
| Tolerances are judgement | 5 percent, 5 degrees Celsius and 15 percentage points set which pairs are comparable. |
| Capture numbers inherit the air-flow unit assumption (A-08) | If the meter reports normal volume, they are off by up to about 12 percent. |
| The independent recompute checks arithmetic | Not the choice of model. |
| Block length 120 s | A much longer correlation time in the residuals would make all intervals too narrow; EXP_005 shows it can happen. |
