## What changed, in plain words

- **A metrics job after cleaning.** `python -m scripts.metrics` reads only the accepted readings and
  derives the numbers. A value exists at an instant only when every input is accepted at that
  instant. Missing inputs lower the coverage; they are never replaced.
- **A gap is not bridged.** Energy and captured CO2 are added up over time. If two readings are
  more than twice their normal spacing apart, the stretch between them is left out of the total and
  out of the covered time. The total is not scaled up to make up for it.
- **Energy is computed twice.** Once from voltage times current, once from the power the plant
  logged. The two are compared as a cross-check.
- **Three places for the results.** Four series in InfluxDB (`metrics_series`), two small tables in
  Postgres, and a summary file plus a readable report in `reports/`.

Details: the formulas, constants, units and rules are one entry each in `docs/metrics.md`. The
constants are one declared table of 10 numbers with a version (`m4-v1`) and a fingerprint test.
Exit codes: 0 written and read back; 1 written and read back but a plausibility check failed;
2 refused, nothing written (including database never created); 3 a query or write failed; 4 written
but the read-back counts differ. New migration `0003_experiment_metrics.sql`.

## What you should look at

Each has a recommendation.

1. **EXP_001 power cross-check is 0.983, not 1.0.** For 120 seconds, from 09:23:20 to 09:25:19 on
   2026-05-04, the logged power is about 6 kW above voltage times current. The cleaning step
   accepted all three signals there (no spike, no stuck stretch). Energy still agrees to 0.67 %.
   The planned threshold was 0.99. You decided: keep 0.99 and report 0.983 as a result. I changed
   no constant and no cleaning rule. Recommendation: keep it as it is, and let the comparison
   milestone decide whether to mask those 120 s.

   Details: 7,005 of 7,125 instants within tolerance (1 % of the product plus 10 W); the largest
   absolute residual is 6,063 W. The other five experiments are 1.0. The plausibility check on
   energy agreement passes (0.0067 against a limit of 0.01). Recorded as a mistake in the planning
   (E-09 in `MISTAKES.md`): the 0.99 was a round number chosen before the data's disagreement had
   been measured.

2. **One label differs from the plan.** The plan said energy per kilogram of CO2 for EXP_002 would
   be complete. The real run gives partial (coverage 0.933). Why: it is computed over the time that
   both power and capture are covered. EXP_002 power has a gap of about 8 minutes (481 to 485 s
   spacing on its hydrolyzer signals), so power coverage is 0.933 while capture coverage is 0.9997.
   Recommendation: keep it. The label is correct; the plan's expectation was wrong.

   Details: every other planned label matches. No constant was changed.

3. **The planned row count was wrong: 269, not 268.** The plan said the fixture would give 268
   capture-rate rows. The construction gives 269: the plan subtracted one excluded reading twice.
   The planned coverage (532 of 600) matches 269. Tests, the fixture README and the summary use 269.
   Recommendation: accept. It is logged (E-09 in `MISTAKES.md`). The plan text and the contract
   example (illustrative values) still say 268.

   Details: 297 efficiency instants, minus the 28 even seconds from +362 to +418 other than +400
   (+400 was already outside the 297).

4. **Assumption A-08: air flow is in actual cubic metres per hour, humidity ignored.** The data
   dictionary does not say whether the air-flow meter reports actual or normal volume. I assumed
   actual. If it is normal, the capture rate, CO2 captured and energy per kilogram are off by the
   ratio of the air density at ambient to the density at standard conditions, up to about 12 %.
   Recommendation: keep the assumption, state it when you present the capture numbers, and ask the
   process owners if you can.

   Details: formula in `docs/metrics.md` (capture rate entry). Rejected: a fixed standard density.
   The effect of humidity was not estimated. Added as A-08 in `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`.

5. **Three experiments have partial capture coverage: EXP_005 (0.75), EXP_004 (0.50), EXP_006
   (0.36).** All three come from calls and gaps already recorded in the cleaning step, not from
   the metrics. Recommendation: accept for now. The comparison milestone decides whether to compare
   on the covered part only.

   Details: EXP_005's first 30 pressure readings are UNCERTAIN, so there is no pressure value for
   the first 30 minutes (1,800 s of 7,200 s), and the capture rate needs pressure. EXP_004's air
   flow was excluded for one hour as a stuck sensor (the EXP_004 air-flow call, A-07), and the capture rate needs air flow
   (3,600 s). EXP_006 has 1,350 stuck outlet-CO2 readings excluded (about 2,700 s) and a gap of
   about 32 minutes on its absorber signals. Filling the barometer was rejected as filling (it
   would change the result by under 0.1 %).

6. **A line-ending fix in `.gitattributes`.** On this Windows machine git converted the migration
   files to CRLF line endings, and the migration runner refused migration 0001, because it stores a
   checksum of the file bytes. I added `migrations/*.sql -text` so git never converts them, and
   recreated 0001 and 0002 from the index. Recommendation: accept; the Linux CI runner and a fresh
   private stack were not affected, only this checkout.

   Details: the recorded checksum for 0001 is `52d6ff5d` (the LF bytes). Migration 0003 was written
   in LF. After the fix `python -m scripts.migrate` applied 0003 on the dev Postgres.

7. **The median residual is signed.** In the power check, `residual_median_w` is the median of the
   signed residual (logged minus computed), while `residual_p95_abs_w` and `residual_max_abs_w` are
   of the absolute residual. So the median can be near 0 even when large errors of both signs
   exist, and the other two never say which side. Recommendation: keep it and read the three
   together. It is stated in `docs/metrics.md`.

8. **Not committed, judge not run, CI not run.** All work is in the working tree on `m4-metrics`.

9. **A report timing line was overwritten and restored.** Reproducing the run on a fresh private
